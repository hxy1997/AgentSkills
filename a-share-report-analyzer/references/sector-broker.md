# Sector Overlay: Broker

Use this reference for securities firms and broker-dealers.

## Why the generic framework is not enough

For brokers, the balance sheet, regulatory capital, funding structure, and market-activity sensitivity are central to the business model.

## Financial-analysis focus

Prioritize:

- business mix: brokerage, investment banking, proprietary trading, institutional business, asset management, margin financing
- earnings sensitivity to capital-market activity
- net capital strength
- risk coverage ratio
- liquidity coverage ratio
- net stable funding ratio
- leverage and funding-cost trend
- client-asset quality and concentration where available

## Profit-quality lens

Look beyond net profit:

- how much comes from recurring fee income versus market-sensitive investment income
- how much of the recovery is cyclical versus structural
- whether cost control improved operating leverage

## Cash-flow and balance-sheet cautions

- do not analyze broker cash flow like a normal industrial company
- financing and client-fund movements can dominate reported flows
- explain why a standard industrial free-cash-flow lens may be misleading

## Valuation reminders

Common anchors:

- `PB` with `ROE`
- cycle-aware `PE`
- historical valuation bands

Use `DCF` only when it adds real clarity. If it is weak, say so.

## Current-regulation reminder

Risk-control rules can change. Verify current regulatory ratios and definitions with up-to-date sources before finalizing.
