# Industry Research Checklist

Use this checklist for the mandatory industry overlay research. This is the preferred payload for the dedicated subagent.

## Required research questions

1. What is the target company's exact sub-industry?
2. What is the sub-industry business model?
3. What are the core operating KPIs that matter more than generic revenue and net profit?
4. Which accounting items or financial ratios are easy to misread in this industry?
5. What valuation methods are commonly used in this sub-industry, and in what situations?
6. What are the main cycle drivers, policy drivers, and competitive forces?
7. Which risk factors should be elevated above the generic framework?
8. Which parts of the generic framework should be downweighted?

## Expected output

The research output should be concise and structured:

- sub-industry identification
- business-model summary
- key KPIs
- accounting or reporting cautions
- valuation anchors
- main risk drivers
- how to modify the generic framework

## Research-source preference

- official disclosures
- high-quality industry reports
- broker reports
- public industry data
- current regulation where relevant

If information could have changed recently, use current browsing rather than relying on memory.

## Example task statement for the subagent

Research the analysis principles for the target company's sub-industry. Focus on business model, key operating metrics, accounting quirks, common valuation anchors, cycle and policy drivers, and how this sub-industry should modify a generic A-share fundamental-analysis framework. Return a concise synthesis suitable for direct insertion into an equity analysis report.
